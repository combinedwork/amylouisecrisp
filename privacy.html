Privacy & Cookie Policy

Mock-up note: the email address below is a placeholder. On WordPress this page is filled in with the site’s real contact email.

This policy explains how I, Amy Louise Crisp, collect and use personal information when you visit amylouisecrisp.co.uk, sign up to my newsletter or get in touch. I keep things simple: I only collect what I need, I never sell your information, and anything optional only happens if you say yes.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who I am

This website is run by Amy Louise Crisp. For anything to do with your personal information, email me at hello@amylouisecrisp.co.uk.

What I collect and why

Newsletter

If you sign up, I collect your email address (and your name if you give it) so I can send my monthly email. My newsletter is sent using an email service provider, which stores your details on my behalf. I rely on your consent, and every email has an unsubscribe link. I keep your details until you unsubscribe.

Emails and messages

If you email me or use a contact form, I use what you send (usually your name, email address and message) to reply. I keep messages for as long as I need to deal with them, and then for a reasonable time in case you get back in touch. This is in my legitimate interest in replying to you.

Visitor statistics (only if you allow them)

With your permission, I use analytics tools such as Google Analytics to see which recipes are popular and how people find the site. This collects information like the pages you visit, the type of device and browser you use, and your approximate location (town or region). It does not tell me who you are. I rely on your consent, which you can change at any time.

Advertising (only if you allow it)

In the future I may show adverts to help keep my recipes free. If I do, and you allow advertising cookies, my advertising partner may use cookies and similar technologies to choose and measure adverts. I will name my advertising partner on this page when adverts start. I rely on your consent.

Recipe videos

Recipe videos are hosted on YouTube. They use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode and only load when you press play and allow videos. YouTube may then set its own cookies, as described in Google’s privacy policy.

Saved recipes and settings

When you save a recipe with the ♡, or choose grid or list view, this is stored only on your own device (in your browser’s storage). It is never sent to me. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser data.

Keeping the website running

Like all websites, my hosting company keeps basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the site secure and working. This is in my legitimate interest in running a safe website.

Who I share information with

I never sell your personal information. I only share it with trusted services that help me run the site, such as my website host, my newsletter provider, Google (for statistics and YouTube) and, if adverts are shown, my advertising partner. They may only use it to provide their service to me.

Some of these services are based outside the UK, for example in the USA. Where this happens, they use approved safeguards (such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK–US data bridge) to protect your information.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information I hold about you
  • ask me to correct or delete it
  • object to, or ask me to limit, how I use it
  • ask for your information in a format you can take elsewhere
  • withdraw your consent at any time (for example, by unsubscribing or changing your cookie settings)

To use any of these rights, email hello@amylouisecrisp.co.uk. I will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. I would always appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Children

My recipes are made for families, but this website is not aimed at children, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has given me their details, please get in touch and I will delete them.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time, for example when I start showing adverts. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
