About Me

Hi, I’m Amy!

I’m a mum of two who loves sharing easy family dinners, delicious baking and lunchbox inspiration.

I believe cooking from scratch doesn’t need to be complicated, expensive or take hours in the kitchen. Here you’ll find simple, family‑friendly recipes you’ll actually want to make again and again!

My kitchen rules

What every recipe has in common

Simple

Easy‑to‑follow steps with no fancy techniques or hard‑to‑find equipment. If I wouldn’t make it on a Tuesday night, it doesn’t go on here.

Affordable

Everyday ingredients you can pick up in the weekly shop, so cooking from scratch doesn’t have to cost more.

Family‑friendly

Food the whole table will actually eat, from fussy little ones to hungry grown‑ups. No hours in the kitchen required.

My story

From busy weeknights to a recipe blog

With two little ones at home, I know how quickly the day disappears, and how tempting it is to reach for something easy. I wanted family meals that were quick, affordable and still made from scratch.

So I started writing down the recipes that worked for us: the dinners everyone finished, the bakes that never lasted long in the tin and the lunchbox ideas that came home empty. This little corner of the internet is where I share them with you.

So grab a cuppa and find something delicious to make!

Amy x

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your recipes suitable for kids?

Yes! Everything here is made with families in mind, using simple ingredients and flavours the whole table can enjoy.

Do I need any special equipment?

Not at all. Most recipes need nothing more than everyday kit like a saucepan, a frying pan and a baking tin. Each recipe lists exactly what you’ll need.

Can I make recipes ahead or freeze them?

Where a recipe can be made ahead or frozen, you’ll find tips under “Tips & Storage” on the recipe card.
