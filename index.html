Creamy Chicken Pesto Pasta

One of those easy family dinners you’ll find yourself making again and again! Packed with flavour, wonderfully creamy and perfect for busy weeknights.

Prep5m
Cook15m
Total20m
Serves4

    Recipe tips

    For a thinner sauce, add a splash of reserved pasta water or extra milk. You can also swap the mushrooms for spinach or leave them out entirely.

    Make ahead

    Chop the chicken, onion and mushrooms in advance and keep them covered in the fridge. Keep raw chicken separate from the vegetables.

    Leftovers

    Cool quickly, then refrigerate in an airtight container within 2 hours. Eat within 48 hours. Reheat until piping hot with a splash of milk. Only reheat once.

    Freezing

    Best enjoyed fresh, as creamy sauces can change texture. If freezing, portion, freeze promptly and defrost fully in the fridge before reheating thoroughly.
